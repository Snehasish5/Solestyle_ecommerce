Returns & Refunds

Our return and refund guidelines for your purchases.

1. Return Window

Eligible products may be returned within 7 days of delivery, provided they meet the conditions mentioned below.

2. Return Conditions

Products must be unused, unworn and returned in their original packaging with all tags and accessories included.

3. Non-Returnable Products

Certain products may not be eligible for return due to hygiene, personalization or other applicable restrictions.

4. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, please contact us as soon as possible with your order information and supporting photographs.

5. Refund Process

Once the returned product has been inspected and approved, eligible refunds will be processed through the original payment method.

6. Refund Timeline

Refund processing time may vary depending on the payment provider or bank.

7. Exchange

Product exchanges may be available depending on stock availability. Please contact customer support for assistance.

8. Contact Us

For return-related questions, please contact our customer support team.
