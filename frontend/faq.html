Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our products, orders and services.

How can I place an order?

Browse our products, select your preferred size and color, add the item to your cart and proceed to checkout.

What payment methods do you accept?

We support major online payment methods including cards, UPI and other available payment options at checkout.

How can I track my order?

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive tracking information through your registered email or phone number.

How long does shipping take?

Standard delivery generally takes a few business days, depending on your location and availability of the product.

Can I cancel my order?

Orders can generally be cancelled before they are shipped. Contact our support team as soon as possible.

Can I return a product?

Yes, eligible products can be returned according to our Returns Policy.

What if I receive a damaged product?

Contact us immediately with your order details and photographs of the damaged product.

How can I contact customer support?

Visit our Contact Us page and send us your query. Our team will get back to you as soon as possible.
